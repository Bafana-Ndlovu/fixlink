# FixLink — Business Overview

## 1. Problem

South African households and small businesses struggle to find reliable home service providers.

- **Trust:** customers can't easily check whether a plumber or electrician is qualified, insured or honest.
- **Price uncertainty:** there is no easy way to know what a job should cost before someone arrives.
- **Discovery:** most hiring happens through WhatsApp groups, Facebook posts and word of mouth, which is slow and hit-and-miss.
- **Provider visibility:** skilled independent providers, especially in townships and smaller suburbs, have no affordable way to market themselves or build a reputation online.

## 2. Solution

FixLink is an online marketplace where customers find, compare and book verified local service providers.

- Search by service and location
- Verified provider profiles (ID and qualification checks)
- Reviews only from completed bookings
- Starting prices shown in Rand
- Quote requests and bookings in one place
- An AI assistant that turns a plain-language problem into the right service category

## 3. Target customers

| Segment | Description | What they want |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homeowners and tenants | Urban and township households in Gauteng first, then Durban and Cape Town | Fast, safe, fairly priced help |
| Landlords and property managers | Manage several units and need repeat maintenance | Reliable providers on call, job history |
| Small businesses | Shops, offices, restaurants | Quick fixes with minimal downtime |
| Service providers (supply side) | Independent tradespeople and small service businesses | More customers, a professional online presence, simple job management |

## 4. Value proposition

- **For customers:** "Find a trusted professional near you in minutes, and know what you'll pay."
- **For providers:** "Get found, get booked and build your reputation, without paying for advertising."

## 5. Revenue model

1. **Commission:** 10% service fee on bookings paid through the platform.
2. **Provider subscription (FixLink Pro):** R199/month for featured placement, more service listings and analytics.
3. **Featured listings:** providers pay to appear at the top of a category in their area.
4. **Later:** partnerships with hardware retailers and insurers (installations, claims repairs).

## 6. Market and competitors

The home services market in South Africa is large and still mostly informal.

| Competitor | What they do | How FixLink differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kandua (owned by Santam since 2024) | Large online home services marketplace | FixLink focuses on township and local providers, a simpler mobile-first experience and an AI assistant for problem diagnosis |
| Snupit | Broad service marketplace across many industries | FixLink is home-services only, with verification and visible starting prices |
| WhatsApp / Facebook groups | Informal recommendations | Verified providers, real reviews, booking history |

## 7. Go-to-market

1. Launch in one area first (Soweto and Johannesburg South) to build density of providers and customers.
2. Onboard providers in person at hardware stores and community centres, free for the first 3 months.
3. Reach customers through community Facebook and WhatsApp groups, local radio and referral codes ("Get R50 off your first booking").
4. Expand to the rest of Gauteng, then Durban and Cape Town.

## 8. Key metrics

- Number of verified providers per area
- Quote requests and bookings per week
- Booking completion rate
- Average provider rating
- Repeat customer rate

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Poor-quality providers damage trust | Verification checks, review-based ranking, removal policy |
| Providers and customers take jobs off-platform | Booking protection and workmanship guarantee only on platform bookings |
| Low early supply in an area | Launch area by area; in-person provider onboarding |
| Data privacy (POPIA) | Collect minimal personal data, clear privacy policy, secure storage |
